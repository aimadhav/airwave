02 · TIME SCALES

“How big are the chunks?” There are several answers.

A frame is a tiny view for measuring sound. A model window is a longer view for understanding it. A recovery core is the interval we are allowed to replace. A context crop includes extra audio to help the model. They are not interchangeable.

See a core inside its context

Illustrative calculator, not inference. Try a 1.46-second overlap with one second of context: up to 3.46 seconds goes into separation, but only the 1.46-second core owns output words.

Actual crops are clipped at the recording edges and before/after another speaker enters. Core splitting does not create overlapping ownership. Context may overlap between jobs; output words may not.

Why the native diarization value is 21.12 s, not 27.2 s

The installed C++ runtime is pinned to v0.2.0. Its v3_offline() preset has 264 coarse frames per chunk at 80 ms/frame: 264 × 0.08 = 21.12 s. Left and right context are one coarse frame each (80 ms). The cache capacity is 264 embedding frames, FIFO is 0, and update period is 188 frames. Cache capacity is compressed speaker memory, not a promise of a contiguous 21-second audio history.

The public V3 prediction grid is 10 ms; the internal chunk/cache grid is 80 ms. The app passes the preset, not a manually chosen chunk length, and does not export resolved runtime geometry into the original run provenance. These values are a version-pinned source verification, not independent per-run telemetry. Do not substitute a newer Python recipe's 340-frame setting. See the primary source below.

03 · THE SIGNAL

Audio is a sequence of measurements.

A microphone turns changing air pressure into a voltage; a digital recording stores regularly spaced measurements of it. One second at 16 kHz has 16,000 samples—not 16,000 words. PCM16 uses a signed 16-bit number per sample; the processing code converts it to floating point by dividing by 32,768.

1. Decode and downmix

FFmpeg converts MP3 to 16 kHz, one-channel, signed PCM16 WAV. The original MP3 remains unchanged. A mono mix discards spatial/channel distinctions, which is convenient but could lose useful separation clues from a genuinely stereo recording.

t = sample index / sample rate
1 s = 16,000 samples = 32,000 PCM bytes

2. Condition the diarization input

The DSP branch subtracts the mean (DC offset) and scales the peak to 0.95. Its local-model resampler uses an antialiasing polyphase filter when the rate changes. This is not a speech-isolation or denoising algorithm.

y[n] = 0.95 · (x[n] − mean(x)) / peak

Whisper and overlap recovery read the decoded processing.wav float samples, not the separate peak-normalized DSP buffer. Their timestamps still refer to the same original session time.

Why 16 kHz, and why separation uses 8 kHz

These installed speech models expect 16 kHz mono. The Nyquist frequency is half the sample rate: frequencies above 8 kHz cannot be retained at 16 kHz. Filtering before downsampling prevents high frequencies from folding into false lower frequencies (aliasing).

The selected SepFormer checkpoint expects 8 kHz input, so overlap crops are antialiased down to 8 kHz and the two outputs are resampled back to 16 kHz, then padded/trimmed to exactly the original crop length. Bandwidth above 4 kHz has been lost: upsampling does not restore it. That can make consonants and voice identity harder to recognize.

The website's 32 kHz / 56 kbps AAC is only a small playback copy of the original MP3. It is not model input, and its higher sample rate does not improve the saved transcription.

04 · VOICE ACTIVITY DETECTION (VAD)

“Is there sound that might be speech?” is not “who spoke?”

The dependency-light energy VAD slides a 30 ms frame (480 samples) forward by 10 ms (160 samples). It computes root-mean-square amplitude, converts it to dB, then marks frames above a threshold. RMS is like a short-window loudness meter, not a language or voice recognizer.

RMS = √(sum(x[n]²) / N)
dB = 20 log₁₀(max(RMS, 10⁻⁸))
threshold = max(−35 dB, 10th-percentile frame level + 3 dB)

The low percentile approximates the background level. Raising the threshold helps avoid treating constant background noise as speech. The policy uses a 200 ms minimum speech region and a 250 ms silence-gap setting; final boundaries are quantized to frames, so these are not exact sample-level cut lengths.

Important: what VAD actually controls here

For the neural local_model provider, energy VAD is diagnostic only. LocalModelDiarizer runs on the full audio and does not hard-gate it with these energy intervals. Whisper's vad_filter is false in this saved run. Changing the energy threshold alone will not fix Nemotron overlap false alarms or Whisper's missing second voice.

Music, laughter, noise, quiet speech and heavily compressed audio can defeat a simple loudness rule. Its confidence is only an energy diagnostic—not a calibrated probability of speech or speaker identity.

Implementation: src/dsp/vad/energy_vad.py · src/dsp/diarization/services/local_provider.py

05 · DIARIZATION

Estimate who is active, without recognizing the words.

DEFAULT VIEW

Nemotron-3 Diarization

A neural diarizer estimates activity for anonymous speaker channels over time. Multiple channels can be active at once: that is why the timeline can represent overlap. The local adapter uses Nemotron-3-Diarization.q8_0.gguf through NeMo-Speech.cpp on CPU with v3-offline and full attention disabled.

The runtime supports up to eight channels; this recording produced five estimated groups. The app renames channels by first occurrence: SPEAKER_00 → Speaker 1. Neither the number nor the label verifies a real identity.

COMPARISON VIEW

Sherpa-ONNX

Pyannote segmentation estimates local voice activity, TitaNet embeddings describe the voices, and clustering groups similar embeddings across the recording. Automatic speaker count is enabled (num_speakers = −1); clustering threshold is 0.9, segmentation shift ratio 0.1, minimum-on 0.2 s and minimum-off 0.3 s.

It produced 69 estimated groups here—fragmentation, not proof of 69 people. The shift ratio is a fraction of the segmentation model's own window, not a 0.1-second chunk. That window duration was not captured in this run's public evidence.

The v3-offline name is easy to misread: it still uses stateful, cached chunks. It is not the --offline full-attention switch. A 57-minute recording uses the long-form path. Native onset/offset and padding values were left to the runtime defaults; exact resolved postprocessing values were not saved. The Sherpa minimum-duration values above must not be mistaken for native overrides.

Model confidence is not exposed by the adapter; 0 in the DSP contract means unavailable, not “0% correct.” Embedding cosine scores later in recovery are a different quantity.

Implementation: src/dsp/diarization/adapters/nemo_native_backend.py · sherpa_backend.py · native v0.2.0/src/asr/diar/aosc_state.h

06 · AUTOMATIC SPEECH RECOGNITION (ASR)

Whisper hears a mixture. It does not automatically give two transcripts.

The baseline runs faster-whisper base.en locally on CPU with int8 computation, four threads, English selected and beam size 5. Beam search compares candidate word sequences; a larger beam is not a speaker detector.

The recognizer transforms sound into a log-mel spectrogram: overlapping 25 ms Fourier-analysis windows (400 samples) at 10 ms steps (160 samples), summarized into 80 mel bands. The Fourier transform describes frequency content; mel bands compress it into a speech-friendly frequency representation. These features are not MFCCs—we do not take the cepstral transform used to form MFCCs here.

samples → windowed FFT → mel-band energies → logarithm → Whisper → words + times

The installed feature extractor has a 30-second model window. The application submits the complete waveform once; faster-whisper advances through it internally, sometimes based on predicted timestamps. This is not a guaranteed tiling of exactly 115 non-overlapping 30-second clips. Individual returned speech segments and word intervals have variable lengths. Word timestamps are model estimates, not hand-checked boundaries.

07 · TEMPORAL ALIGNMENT

Attach each recognized word to the best overlapping voice interval.

For each word interval, the app measures how much it overlaps each speaker's activity. It takes the union of a speaker's intersecting intervals so duplicates cannot inflate their score, then chooses the speaker with greatest covered time. Ties are broken deterministically by speaker ID.

coverage(speaker, word) = duration(word ∩ union(speaker's intervals))
assigned speaker = largest coverage
uncertain if more than one speaker competes OR coverage / word duration < 0.5

If there is no speaker interval, the word becomes an unassigned row. An active speaker with no recovered words stays visible as “words unresolved.” This is temporal reconciliation, not acoustic proof that the chosen person said those words.

Intersecting turns are grouped into passages and stacked one below another. A passage can include a chain of overlaps; it does not mean everybody speaks simultaneously for its entire duration. Merely touching endpoints do not count as overlap. The 93 displayed overlap passages and the 470 recovery cores are therefore different counts.

Implementation: src/recording_viewer/services/alignment.py · web/recording-viewer/viewer.js

08 · OVERLAP RECOVERY

A cautious second pass, only where voices collide.

  1. Find ownership cores

    A sweep-line algorithm visits every speaker start/end boundary and counts distinct active speakers. Regions with at least two speakers become candidates. Longer regions are split into cores no longer than 6 s. Context extends up to 1 s on either side, clipped to the file and to exclude a third speaker outside the core.

  2. Build clean voice references

    Find non-overlapping, one-speaker spans. Trim 0.15 s from both edges; cut 2 s reference clips. Keep up to five spread across the recording, and require at least two successful embeddings for a profile. Normalize the vectors, average them, then normalize the mean. These are within-session anonymous profiles, not registered people.

  3. Check eligibility and budget

    Exactly two active speakers, core at least 0.3 s, sufficient clean references, and an available attempt budget are required. The current budget is 40 attempted windows in chronological order. Failed or rejected attempts also consume it. A two-output separator cannot honestly solve three simultaneous voices.

  4. Separate into two waveforms

    SpeechBrain sepformer-libri2mix estimates two sources from the mixed crop. It uses a learned waveform encoder, Transformer-based masking and a waveform decoder; it is not just a high/low-frequency filter. Both speakers occupy overlapping frequencies. The checkpoint is trained for two-source 8 kHz speech, not guaranteed to isolate a podcast with crosstalk, laughter, compression or more voices.

  5. Correct unsafe decoder gain

    The newer adapter applies one attenuation factor to both tracks if their peak exceeds 0.98. It never independently boosts a faint track. This preserves the relative levels and avoids feeding out-of-range values to recognition or embeddings. This fix is in the later listening examples, not the older full-run recovery artifact.

    p = max absolute sample across both tracks
    g = min(1, 0.98 / p) · both tracks multiplied by g

  6. Match voices and recognize each track

    Signal checks run on the core. Identity embeddings use the whole permitted crop; at least 0.6 s is required. References and separated audio are both down/up-resampled to the separator bandwidth before TitaNet embedding, to compare like with like. Two possible one-to-one voice assignments are scored by cosine similarity, with a per-track similarity and margin requirement.

    Whisper transcribes each separated crop independently. A word belongs to the core only if its midpoint lies inside the half-open interval [core start, core end). Context words are not inserted. Absolute word times are restored by adding the sample-aligned crop start; full word intervals can straddle the core boundary.

  7. Promote only if every gate passes

    Both tracks need owned words, sufficient model confidence, distinct voice assignments and no long near-duplicate text. Accepted cores replace baseline words by midpoint ownership; rejected or skipped cores retain the uncertain baseline. Original evidence is not deleted. “Accepted” is an engineering decision, not human-verified truth.

Cosine similarity in plain language, and why it is not a probability

An embedding is a numerical description of a voice. Cosine measures how similarly two vectors point: cos(a,b) = (a·b) / (||a|| ||b||). Matching requires at least 0.45 for each assigned voice and an advantage of at least 0.05 over the other candidate. A 0.60 score does not mean 60% identity accuracy.

The one-to-one assignment prevents both tracks from taking the same speaker label. But the candidate set is restricted to the two diarized speakers, and a noisy reference, wrong diarization pair or cross-track leakage can still produce a misleading match.

Implementation: recovery_timeline.py · overlap_recovery.py · recovery_quality.py · adapters/voice_embedder.py · scripts/separate_overlap_worker.py

Inside SepFormer: the smaller chunks inside a recovery crop

The inspected installed architecture uses 256 learned encoder channels, a 16-sample kernel and an 8-sample stride at 8 kHz: a 2 ms receptive window stepping every 1 ms. Encoded features are grouped into 250-step chunks at a 125-step hop—roughly 250 ms chunks with 50% overlap, not 250 seconds. Exact receptive-field coverage includes the encoder's window edges.

Two dual-path blocks apply an intra-chunk Transformer for local structure and an inter-chunk Transformer for relationships across those feature chunks. Each path has eight layers and eight attention heads. Learned masks select which encoded features belong to each of two sources; a decoder reconstructs waveforms, combining overlapping feature chunks. These architecture values come from the hash-checked installed hyperparams.yaml and SpeechBrain implementation, not the web UI's chunk slider.

The model is processing the whole permitted recovery crop. Its 250-step internal feature chunks must not be confused with the application's maximum 6-second output-ownership core or the current maximum 8-second context crop.

Reconstruction, correlation and duplicate text: the math

Track RMS is √mean(track²). The energy ratio is min(mean(track₁²),mean(track₂²)) / max(...). Correlation is the absolute normalized dot product after subtracting each track's mean. Two nearly identical outputs suggest duplicated sources.

Reconstruction solves a least-squares fit x ≈ a·track₁ + b·track₂, then computes 10 log₁₀((mean(x²)+ε)/(mean(residual²)+ε)). Adjustable gains are intentional; a good reconstruction still does not prove clean separation. This is not reference-based SI-SDR or a measured speech-quality score.

Duplicate text uses lowercased word tokens and Python SequenceMatcher. The check applies only if each track has at least three words. Short duplicated acknowledgements or different words from the same leaking voice may escape it.

11 · A FIXING GUIDE, NOT AN ACCURACY PROMISE
Where to spend the next engineering effort.
01 / COVERAGE
Stop confusing “not attempted” with “couldn't recover.”
287 cores were skipped because the 40-attempt budget was exhausted. The last attempt ended at 6:18.39 in a 57:18.62 file. That is a chronological prefix bias: later conversations were never tested by that full-run recovery job.
Next experiment: rerun with the gain-fixed adapter, logged policy and a budget covering eligible windows—or a predeclared, stratified sample spread over the recording. Preserve the original as a baseline. Report attempt coverage separately from accepted count.
02 / SPEAKER IDENTITY
Inspect the 32 ambiguous matches.
Wrong boundaries, short contaminated crops and the 8 kHz separator's lost bandwidth can distort voice vectors. Dropping the cosine threshold may accept more wrong speakers.
Next experiment: compare reference consistency, wrong-speaker negatives, nearby clean references, larger/cleaner crops and a compatible 16 kHz separator. Evaluate identity accuracy on held-out human labels, not just gate pass rate.
03 / FALSE OVERLAPS
Validate the detector before separating every alert.
The small 12-case listening review contained three true overlaps and nine non-overlap controls. This convenience sample is not an overall detector precision/recall estimate. A clean single voice split into two tracks can still fool confidence checks.
Next experiment: calibrate native onset/offset and boundary rules on labelled data; include random negatives and missed-overlap searches. Explicitly log resolved runtime defaults. Do not try to solve this only by changing the diagnostic energy VAD.
04 / WORDS & LEAKAGE
Test stronger ASR after checking the source tracks.
Five attempted cores failed recognition-confidence checks; one recovered no secondary words. Some “accepted” later clips still have audible cross-track leakage. A larger recognizer can also confidently transcribe leaked speech.
Next experiment: compare base.en and small.en on the same corrected tracks and mixed baseline, using checked per-speaker words. Keep the separator, context and scoring core fixed for a fair comparison; then vary context separately.
05 / THREE VOICES & SHORT REPLIES
Make unsupported cases explicit.
39 cores had unsupported simultaneous speaker counts; 104 were below the 0.3 s threshold. Neither is evidence of successful recovery. Context does not turn a two-output separator into a three-person model.
Next experiment: evaluate a real multi-source or target-speaker extraction provider before advertising that capability. For brief replies, test different core/context policies without inventing words or borrowing words from outside the scoring interval.
06 / MEASUREMENT
Measure correctness, not just acceptance.
The gain-fixed one-second-context experiment passed 2/3 reviewed true overlaps and wrongly accepted 1/9 controls, compared with 0/3 and 2/9 before correction. Three-second context was tested only on positives. These are small gate outcomes, not recording-wide accuracy.
Next experiment: use separate tuning and held-out sets; report per-speaker WER, diarization error (with the overlap/collar protocol stated), overlap precision/recall, leakage listening checks, runtime and acceptance coverage. Without isolated clean ground-truth sources, do not claim reference-based separation scores.
