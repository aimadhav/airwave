Speaker labels and words are estimates. Full-recording results and the later gain-fix clip experiment are different snapshots; neither is a claim of perfect transcription.

What ran on the whole recording?

The default transcript view uses the original Nemotron timeline. The older overlap-recovery snapshot is retained for comparison, not presented as a new full-recording run after the gain fix.

What changed after correcting separator gain?

A small listening review found 3 true overlaps and 9 non-overlap controls among 12 detected candidates. Gate acceptance measures automatic checks, not word accuracy.

The three-second context diagnostic tested only the three true overlaps. Its false-acceptance rate was not measured. These results do not establish recording-wide accuracy or missed-overlap rate.

Listen to the three corrected examples

Original mixture and separated tracks cover the same samples. Displayed hypotheses cover only the scoring core; context outside it can contain additional speech. Faint voice leakage can remain.

Limitations and attribution

Anonymous voice groups are not verified people. Overlap detection has false alarms, recognition can miss or invent words, and separated tracks can contain leakage. Rejected examples remain visible instead of being described as successfully recovered speech.

This bundle is a frozen demonstration, not an upload server. It needs no API key and makes no calls to a localhost backend. Podcast ownership remains with its rights holders; the source-code license does not grant rights to the audio.
